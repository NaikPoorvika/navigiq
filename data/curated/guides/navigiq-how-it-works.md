# How NavigIQ works and what it does not do

NavigIQ is an exploration companion for Bengaluru and places within about 90 kilometres of the city centre. It helps you discover places, understand them and plan a day around them.

== Where the place data comes from ==
Place names, locations, categories and opening hours come from OpenStreetMap, which is open data published under the Open Database License (ODbL). Descriptions come from Wikipedia under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence where an article exists, and from Wikidata. NavigIQ adds its own editorial notes, such as experience tags and suggested visit lengths, for a set of places it has curated. Every place page lists its sources.

== Ratings ==
NavigIQ does not show star ratings. There is no free and lawful source of ratings, so NavigIQ never invents them. Rankings use how well a place matches your request, how well the place is documented, and NavigIQ's own curation.

== Costs ==
Costs are estimates per person for entry, tickets, activities or food, shown as a range with a note on how the estimate was made. Some estimates come from a fee or charge recorded in OpenStreetMap, some from NavigIQ's editorial notes, and the rest from typical costs for that kind of place. Transport costs are not included. Shopping and other discretionary spending are not estimated.

== Opening hours ==
Opening hours are shown as verified only when they come from a parsed OpenStreetMap record or NavigIQ's curation. Many places have no published hours; for those NavigIQ uses a typical window for that kind of place and treats it as a soft guide, never as a promise. Always check hours before a special trip.

== Transportation ==
This version of NavigIQ does not calculate travel time, routes, traffic or fares. Plans include a fixed transition buffer between stops, which is not a travel time. Plans keep stops geographically close together, but how you get between them is up to you.

== Weather ==
When a plan's date is within the forecast range, NavigIQ checks the Open-Meteo forecast and prefers indoor places if rain is likely. If the forecast is unavailable, plans are made without weather adjustments and NavigIQ says so.

== The 90 kilometre region ==
NavigIQ's discovery region is everything within 90 kilometres of the city centre, measured in a straight line. It is grouped into the city core, the wider city, the outskirts and nearby escapes. Searches near a neighbourhood stay local; only requests for escapes or places outside the city reach the outer bands. The distance bands describe where a place is, not how long it takes to get there.
